# Privacy and Data Processing Policy

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Gdpr
Corvane acts as a processor under GDPR and signs a Data Processing Agreement with Standard Contractual Clauses for any transfer outside the EEA.

## Deletion request
Individual records can be deleted on request through the API, and data subject requests are completed within 30 days.

## Offshore
Customer data is stored only in the selected region. Support staff in the US, UK, and Portugal may access data with customer permission.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

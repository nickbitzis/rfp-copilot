# Pricing and Plans

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Storage limits
Workspaces on the Business plan include 500 GB of storage and Enterprise plans include 5 TB, with additional storage billed per TB.

## Pricing model
Corvane Insight is priced per named user per year, with 3 plans: Team, Business, and Enterprise. Volume discounts start at 100 users.

## Trial
A 14 day free trial of the Business plan is available with sample data.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

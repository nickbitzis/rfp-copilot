# Master Subscription Agreement Summary

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Audit rights
Customers may review our SOC 2 report and send one security questionnaire per year. On-site audits are available to Enterprise customers with 30 days notice.

## Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice if the breach is not cured, and prepaid fees are refunded pro rata when Corvane is in breach.

## Liability
Liability is capped at 12 months of fees paid. Uncapped liability is not offered, but a higher cap of 2 times annual fees for data breaches is available on Enterprise agreements.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

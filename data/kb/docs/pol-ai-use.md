# AI Use and Model Data Policy

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Ai training
Corvane does not use customer data to train machine learning models, and no customer data is sent to a third-party model provider unless the customer enables the AI assistant.

## Ai assistant
The optional AI assistant answers questions about dashboards. It is off by default, an administrator must enable it, and prompts are not retained by the model provider.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

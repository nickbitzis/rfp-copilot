# Incident Response Plan

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Ir plan
Corvane maintains a documented incident response plan with defined severity levels, an on-call rotation, and a tabletop exercise every 6 months.

## Breach notification
Corvane notifies affected customers of a confirmed security breach involving their data within 48 hours.

## Breach history
Corvane has not had a security breach involving customer data in the last 5 years.

## Forensics
Corvane keeps an external incident response firm on retainer and preserves forensic evidence for at least 12 months after an incident.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

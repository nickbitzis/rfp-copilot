# Support Policy

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Support hours
Standard support is available 9am to 6pm on business days. Enterprise customers get 24x7 support for Severity 1 issues.

## Response times
Severity 1 issues receive a response within 1 hour for Enterprise customers and within 4 hours for Business customers.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

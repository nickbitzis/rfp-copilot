# Key Management Standard

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Key mgmt
Encryption keys are managed in a cloud key management service backed by FIPS 140-2 Level 3 hardware security modules and are rotated every 12 months.

## Byok
Customer managed keys (BYOK) are available on the Enterprise plan for data at rest in the US and EU regions. Hold your own key is not supported.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

# Logging and Monitoring Standard

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Log retention
Security and audit logs are retained for 13 months, with the most recent 90 days searchable online.

## Monitoring
Security events are monitored 24 hours a day by an internal security operations team using a SIEM with automated alerting.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

# Personnel Security Policy

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Background checks
All employees and contractors pass a background check before they start, where local law allows.

## Security training
All staff complete security awareness training at hire and every 12 months, engineers complete secure coding training every 12 months, and phishing simulations run monthly.

## Confidentiality
All employees and contractors sign confidentiality agreements before they receive system access.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Backups
Customer data is backed up every 4 hours. Backups are encrypted, stored in a second region, and retained for 35 days.

## Rpo rto
The recovery point objective is 4 hours and the recovery time objective is 8 hours for a full region failure.

## Dr test
The disaster recovery plan is tested every 12 months with a full regional failover exercise. The last test was completed on 2026-04-14.

## Bcp
Corvane maintains a business continuity plan, reviewed every 12 months, that covers loss of offices, key staff, and critical vendors.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

# Service Level Agreement

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Sla uptime
Corvane commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for the Business plan and 99.95% for the Enterprise plan, with service credits of up to 25% of monthly fees.

## Maintenance
Planned maintenance is announced at least 5 business days in advance and is scheduled on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC. Most releases need no downtime.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.

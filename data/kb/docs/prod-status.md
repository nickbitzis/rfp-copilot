# Status Page and Availability

## Purpose
This document sets the rules described below. It applies to all employees, contractors, and production systems.

## Uptime history
Measured availability over the last 12 months was 99.97%. Historical uptime is published on the public status page.

## Status page
A public status page shows current availability and incident history, and customers can subscribe to email or webhook notifications.

## Ownership and review
The owner of this document is the security-lead. It is reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the platform.
